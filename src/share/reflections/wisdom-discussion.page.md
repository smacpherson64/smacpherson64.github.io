# Starting Topic: 

Proverbs and wise sayings

> **Question**
>
> Why do proverbs and wise sayings impact us so deeply?

To get started, here is a pity modern-(ish) proverb that packs a punch the longer one thinks about it:

> A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.

It’s pragmatic and useful but also has meaning far beyond the immediate. 

> **Question**
>
>
> How can such a simple statement be so potent?

Proverbs and wise sayings seem to be plentiful and ubiquitous. 

The topics have a large range, the styles are quite varied, and they come from many different cultures...

Some are similar but distinct:

> - A spark can start a great fire
> - A journey of 1000 miles starts with one step
> - A little each day is a lot in a year

Some are conflicting:

> - One false move may lose the game
> - A man too careful lives in constant torment

Some are deep and straightforward:

> - Every man has his price
> - Appearances are deceptive
> - There is no greater love […] than laying one’s life down for one’s friends.

Some are deep and cryptic:

> - Milk the cow but don’t pull off the udder

Some are kind and compassionate like Buddhist teachings

Some are deadly like The Art of War

Some are idealistic and foreign like the Christian's book of Proverbs

Some are pessimistic like the Christian's book of Ecclesiastes

Some are even ancient to the ancients:

> - Hatreds never cease by hatreds in this world. By love alone they cease. This is an ancient Law.

Some are brand new:

> - Go touch some grass
